long

Kate Solmara is an Arizona-based singer-songwriter and mother of two whose music is shaped by a deep interest in identity, surrender, resilience, and what it means to become more fully yourself. She released her debut single, “Ease,” at 39, beginning her work as Kate Solmara with a warm, emotionally restorative indie folk-pop sound.

Her songwriting is intimate and reflective, often exploring the tension between striving and rest, self-protection and trust, and the courage it takes to choose a life that feels true. Produced by Adam Lamb, “Ease” has found radio support, independent playlist placements and press coverage, with listeners in more than 100 countries.
