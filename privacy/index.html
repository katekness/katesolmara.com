Privacy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Kate Solmara respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected through katesolmara.com and how it may be used.

Information you provide

If you contact me through the website, I may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, the station, outlet or company you represent, and your message.

If you join the Solmara Circle, I collect your first name and email address, and, if you choose to share it, where you are listening from, so I can send you updates about new music, releases, projects and related news. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any email or writing to info@katesolmara.com.

Forms

The contact and Solmara Circle forms are provided by Tally. Information submitted through these forms is processed and stored by Tally in accordance with its own privacy practices. I do not sell or rent your information to advertisers or other third parties.

Payments and artist support

This website links to PayPal for people who choose to financially support the music. Payments are processed directly by PayPal. This website does not process or store your payment-card information. PayPal may provide certain transaction information necessary to process and document the payment.

Third-party services and embedded content

This website includes links to or embedded content from third-party platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Instagram, Bandcamp, SoundCloud, PayPal, Tally and Google Fonts. Those services may collect technical or usage information when you interact with their content. Their collection and use of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Website and technical information

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and other service providers necessary to operate the website may process limited technical information, such as IP address, browser or device information, and basic usage or security data. This site does not use its own analytics or advertising cookies. If analytics or other tracking tools are added in the future, this policy will be updated.

How information is used

Information collected through this site may be used to respond to inquiries; send requested music and artist updates; manage Solmara Circle subscriptions; process artist-support transactions through third-party payment providers; operate, maintain and secure the website; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Data retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to inquiries, maintaining Solmara Circle subscriptions, and meeting legitimate business or legal recordkeeping requirements.

Your choices

You may request to access, correct or delete personal information you have provided directly to me, subject to any information that must be retained for legitimate legal or administrative purposes. You may also unsubscribe from email communications at any time.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the website, services or privacy practices change. The date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or information you have submitted through the site, please contact Kate Solmara at info@katesolmara.com.
